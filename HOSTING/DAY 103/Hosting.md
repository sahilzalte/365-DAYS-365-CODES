## Types of Hosting

1. **Shared Hosting**: Ideal for small websites with low traffic. Resources are shared among multiple users, which makes it cost-effective.
2. **Dedicated Hosting**: Best for large businesses with high traffic. Users have full control over the server, offering better performance and security.
3. **Managed Hosting**: The hosting provider handles server management tasks, allowing users to focus on their website or application.
4. **Cloud Hosting**: Scalable and flexible solution that uses multiple servers to host websites, ensuring high availability and reliability.
